A cleaner built for the bay.

Spark is a professional-strength brake and parts cleaner, supplied from Mississauga, Ontario to workshops, fleets and distributors.

One job, done properly.

Brake work leaves fluid, grease and dust on parts that have to go back on clean. Spark was made for that moment: a non-chlorinated, low VOC cleaner that cuts the contamination and flashes off without leaving anything behind.

We keep it simple. One 550 ml can, one clear set of directions, and the same result whether it is used in a fleet depot, an independent garage or a home workshop.

New to brake cleaner, or moving away from a chlorinated product? Our brake cleaner guide explains the difference and walks through the job step by step.
